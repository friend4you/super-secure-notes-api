Frequently asked questions

Account & login

How do I create an account?
Register in the app with your email and password.
I forgot my password — can you reset it?
No. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes; we cannot recover them. Your vault and notes are encrypted with keys derived from your password. If you lose your password and do not have access on another device, your encrypted data cannot be recovered. You must create a new account if you have no local backup.
How do I delete my account?
Open the app → Settings → Delete Account. Enter your password to confirm. This permanently deletes your account and all server-side data.

Notes & sync

Are my notes readable on your servers?
No. Notes and attachments are encrypted on your device before upload. We store encrypted blobs and minimal sync metadata only.
Notes not syncing?
Check your internet connection, unlock the vault, and try again. The first request after a period of inactivity may take longer while the service starts (Render cold start).
I deleted a note — can I recover it?
Only if it still exists on another device that has not synced the deletion.

Sharing

How does sharing work?
Enter the recipient’s registered email address. The note is encrypted for their key and shared read-only. Only you can edit the note.
Recipient not found?
They must have a Super Secure Notes account registered with that exact email address. Sharing is private and encrypted end-to-end between accounts.
